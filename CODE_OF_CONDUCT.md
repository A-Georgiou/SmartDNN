# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

SmartDNN is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone who participates in the project community. We want contributors, users, and maintainers to collaborate in a constructive environment regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity or orientation.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that help create a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate in discussions, reviews, and feedback
- Assuming good intent and engaging in constructive technical debate
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Accepting accountability and learning from mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the broader community

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or hateful conduct of any kind
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments
- Publishing or threatening to publish private information without permission
- Trolling, intimidation, or sustained disruption of discussions
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and other public or private interactions related to SmartDNN.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainer at [AndrewGeorgiou98@outlook.com](mailto:AndrewGeorgiou98@outlook.com). Please include relevant details such as what happened, when it occurred, and any supporting context that may help with review.

All reports will be reviewed and handled as promptly and confidentially as possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate in response to behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, including warning, temporary suspension, or permanent removal from project spaces.
